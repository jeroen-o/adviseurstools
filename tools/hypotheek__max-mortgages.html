Marital status
Your marital status determines whether your partner's income is included. If married or cohabiting, you can enter your partner's income below.
Pension test from age
From this age (as long as the state pension age has not yet been reached) the mortgage is also tested on the income from state pension age (AOW + pension) using the AOW table; the lower outcome applies. Default 57 (Nibud / Dutch Temporary Mortgage Credit Regulation); some lenders already apply this test from age 52.
Date of birth
Optional — with a date of birth, the state pension status below is set automatically.
State pension age reached *
Choose:

• Yes – you have reached the Dutch state pension age (67 in 2026–2027; 67 years and 3 months for those reaching it in 2028–2031).
• 57+, not yet AOW – you have reached the selected pension test age but not yet the state pension age. The test is then run on your current income and on your income from state pension age (AOW table); the lower outcome applies.
• No – neither applies.

With a date of birth this is determined automatically (state pension age 67 if born up to 31-12-1960, otherwise 67 years and 3 months — indicative after 2031).

The purchase price or appraised value of the property (after any renovation). You may borrow up to 100% of the market value. Leave blank if you do not yet know the value.
Energy label of the property
The energy label determines whether you can borrow extra for energy-saving measures. A better label allows a higher maximum mortgage at purchase and potentially more for renovation.
Mortgage interest rate *
%
The interest rate on your mortgage. For fixed-rate periods under 10 years, Dutch law requires a minimum test rate of 5% (AFM norm), even if your actual rate is lower.
Fixed-rate period
With ≥ 10 years fixed rate, the actual rate is used. With < 10 years, the minimum test rate of 5% applies (AFM norm).
Mortgage interest deductible in box 1
Mortgage interest is deductible in box 1 if the mortgage is repaid on an annuity or linear basis (mandatory for new mortgages since 2013). Choose 'partly' if only part is deductible.

€
The portion of the mortgage not deductible in box 1, e.g. an interest-only part taken out before 2013.

Monthly payment obligation for instalment credit. Reduces the maximum mortgage.
Other credit (revolving / credit card)
€
Outstanding balance on revolving credit or credit card limit. 2% per month of this amount is counted as a mortgage obligation.
Student loan monthly payment
€/month
Actual monthly DUO payment (interest + repayment). Under the Trhk 2026 it is multiplied by a gross-up factor that depends on the test rate (1.05 at ≤ 2% up to 1.40 from 6.001%; 1.25 at 4.5%). Factor 1 if the mortgage interest is not deductible.
Spousal support you pay
€/month
Spousal support (alimony) you pay per month. Counted as a gross monthly obligation and deducted from the permitted monthly mortgage burden (not grossed up, as it is tax-deductible).
Spousal support your partner pays
€/month
Spousal support your partner pays per month. Counted as a gross monthly obligation (not grossed up).

Monthly ground rent (canon) for leasehold land. Counted as a gross monthly obligation and deducted from the permitted monthly burden (not grossed up, as it is deductible in box 1).
